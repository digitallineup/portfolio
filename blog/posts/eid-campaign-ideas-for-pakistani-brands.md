## Quick answers

### What makes an Eid campaign work in Pakistan?

A real product inside a real Eid moment, with a clear way to buy. Our Shangrilla Eid ul Adha film does not show a moon or a lantern. It shows chai being poured, mithai boxes being opened and an Eid cake being cut at one family table. People recognise their own Eid, and the bakery is in the middle of it.

### When should a brand start planning its Eid campaign?

Plan in Ramadan for Eid ul Fitr and at least a month ahead for Eid ul Adha. The shoot, the edit, the ad approvals and the stock all take time, and the week before Eid is when people decide where to buy. If your content is still being made that week, you have missed the window.

### Should Eid posts sell or just greet?

Both, in that order of effort. One warm greeting is fine. The rest of the calendar should give people a reason to act: a gift box, an Eid menu, a pre-order cut-off, a WhatsApp number. Taimoor Cattle Farm's Eid video started 736 WhatsApp conversations because it ended with a way to buy, not a wish.

## The Eid campaign we are proudest of: one family table

For Eid ul Adha, Shangrilla Cuisine Bakers had a problem every bakery shares. Eid ul Adha is about qurbani and meat. Nobody plans their Eid ul Adha around cake. Competing with the BBQ was never going to work.

So we did not compete with it. The film opens in black and white on a grill, smoke still rising, with one line on screen: "When the grill cools down... the real Eid moments begin." Then it cuts to colour. Chai pours into a cup. Hands reach into a tray of mithai. Shangrilla gift boxes stack up on the table. Someone cuts an Eid cake with a crescent on top, and someone else takes a photo of the spread on their phone. It closes on a family round the table with the line "Eid moments, made sweeter by Shangrilla."

It runs about 40 seconds and every shot has a job:

- **The hook admits the obvious.** Eid ul Adha is about the grill. Saying so in the first frame earns trust and sets up the turn.
- **The product is the second half of the day.** The bakery claims the evening, when guests arrive and the chai comes out. That moment had no owner.
- **Gift boxes are on screen early.** Eid is a gifting season, and the packaging is what people carry to relatives. Showing it sells it.
- **The phone shot is the social proof.** People photograph a beautiful Eid table. The film shows that, so it feels like their Eid, not an ad.

We ran a Meta campaign around the film. You can watch it on the [Shangrilla Cuisine Bakers case study](/work/shangrilla-bakers.html) and on our [video production](/video-production-company/) page, where it sits under festival films.

## Eid campaign ideas for Pakistani brands that actually sell

Here are the ideas we come back to. Each one is built on something we have made or run.

### 1. Own the moment no one else owns

Every food brand fights over Eid morning. Look for the hour nobody claims. For a bakery on Eid ul Adha it was the evening after the BBQ. For a clothing brand it might be chand raat. For a solar company it could be the guests arriving to a house with the lights still on. Name the moment in one line and build the film around it.

### 2. Sell the gift, not the greeting

Eid is a gifting season. Mithai boxes, cake, biscuits, dry fruit, clothes, eidi. Shoot your gift packaging properly, show it being handed over, and give a clear pre-order cut-off. Shangrilla's packaging appears in the first seconds of the Eid film for that reason.

### 3. Shoot one film, cut it two ways

Taimoor Cattle Farm wanted to sell animals before Eid with buyers messaging on WhatsApp. We made one film of the farm and cut it twice. An engagement version warmed up the audience and earned 105K engagements at Rs 0.07 each. A WhatsApp version did the selling: 736 conversations at Rs 26 each. One film, two jobs.

### 4. Make Ramadan the runway

Eid ul Fitr sales start in Ramadan. Shangrilla Gardens & BBQ ran a Ramadan iftar menu campaign, "Shandaar iftar, behtareen zaiqa", built on our own menu photography. One Ramadan video for Shangrilla passed 458K engagements. The audience you build in Ramadan is the audience you sell to on chand raat.

### 5. Use your real product, shot properly

Stock photos of lanterns and generic food are everywhere at Eid. Your actual Eid cake, your actual biryani deg, your actual gift box, lit and styled, stands out in a feed full of templates. Every Shangrilla Eid and Ramadan post runs on photography from our shoots, not stock.

### 6. Give WhatsApp the last word

In Pakistan, an Eid order usually starts with a chat. Point your Eid ads at WhatsApp, have someone ready to reply in the final week, and keep a short price list ready to send. That is how the cattle farm turned one video into 736 buyer conversations.

## Eid social media posts: what to put on the calendar

A strong Eid calendar is not one post on Eid morning. For a food or gifting brand, it usually looks like this:

- **Ramadan weeks:** iftar and sehri menus, deals for each branch, and short films of the food being made.
- **Two weeks out:** the Eid gift box or Eid menu reveal, with pre-order details.
- **The final week:** reminders with the cut-off date, behind-the-scenes from the kitchen, and the main Eid film running as an ad.
- **Chand raat:** the busiest night for sweets and last-minute gifts. Post the opening hours and the WhatsApp number.
- **Eid day:** one warm greeting that still looks like your brand, plus stories of real orders going out.
- **Days two and three:** guests are still visiting. Keep selling the gift box and the cake.

Shangrilla Gardens & BBQ runs on a 12-post monthly calendar, every post shot by us, and Ramadan and Eid are planned into it months ahead. Our [social media management](/social-media-marketing-agency/) page explains how we build a calendar like this around a brand's offers and festivals.

Every one starts with a real product and a real feeling, not a template.

## What to take away

- **Skip the template greeting.** A moon and a gold border will not sell anything.
- **Find the Eid moment nobody owns.** Shangrilla claimed the evening after the BBQ.
- **Sell the gift.** Packaging on screen, pre-order dates in the caption.
- **Shoot once, cut twice.** One film became a warm-up cut and a WhatsApp cut for Taimoor Cattle Farm.
- **Start in Ramadan.** One Shangrilla Ramadan video passed 458K engagements.
- **End on WhatsApp.** 736 buyer chats at Rs 26 each came from one video.
- **Plan early.** Shoot weeks ahead, so the final week is for selling, not editing.
