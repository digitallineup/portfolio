## How to judge any marketing agency in Multan

Use this on us too. Ask for:

1. **Three results with numbers**, not a showreel. Followers, leads, cost per conversation, views. If they cannot show a number, they were not watching one.
2. **Who shoots and who designs.** Names, not "our team". If the answer is "we have partners", budget for delays.
3. **Ownership of the ad account.** You should own it and pay Meta directly. An agency that keeps the account keeps your data when you leave.
4. **One contact who answers on the same day.** Pakistan runs on WhatsApp. An agency that replies on Tuesday to a Friday message will reply the same way when a campaign is failing.
5. **Reviews you can click.** We have 16 on Google at 5.0, plus profiles on Clutch, Sortlist and GoodFirms. Read the bad ones at any agency first.

Price last. A cheap monthly fee with no shoots, no ad design and no report costs more than a proper one, because the months pass and nothing moves.
