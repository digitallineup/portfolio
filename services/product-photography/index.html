FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp. A person replies, not a bot.

What kind of products do you photograph?

Anything a customer buys by looking at it first: electronics and appliances, batteries and solar products, sanitaryware and tiles, textiles and clothing, packaged food, cosmetics and more. We shoot in our studio or on location in your showroom or factory.

Do you do white background ecommerce photos?

Yes. We shoot clean packshots on white or a seamless colour for Daraz, Amazon, Shopify and your own website, with consistent lighting and angles across the whole catalogue.

What are 3D-style product visuals?

Scenes where the real product sits in a room, a city or an idea that would be expensive to build for real, like an AC cooling a whole street or a battery in a sunlit lounge. We combine the product photo with a designed scene so it still looks like your actual product.

Can you shoot products sent from abroad?

Yes. Brands outside Pakistan can courier products to our Multan studio. We shoot to your brief and send the files digitally, ready for your store and ads.

How much does product photography cost?

Our packages start from PKR 120,000. The price depends on the number of products, angles per product, and whether you need lifestyle scenes, 3D-style composites or product films. Send the form and we will quote a fixed price.

Do you also make product videos?

Yes. We shoot short product films for reels and ads on the same set: reveals, spins, close-ups of texture and finish, and quick before-and-after cuts.
