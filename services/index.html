FAQ

Questions, answered.

What services does Digital Lineup offer?

Eight, all made in-house in our Multan studio: food photography, product photography, video production, creator and influencer campaigns, Meta ads, social media management, branding and identity, and website design and development.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

Yes. We work with brands in any country. Briefs, concept reviews and reporting run over video calls and shared boards in your time zone, and every file is delivered digitally, ready for print and screen wherever you are.

Can one team handle the shoot, the design and the ads?

Yes, and that is the point of the studio. The people who plan the campaign also shoot it, design it and run the ads, so nothing gets lost between the idea and the ad account.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. You pay Meta or Google directly from your own ad account, and our fee covers the work. That way you can see exactly where every rupee or dollar goes.

How do we get started?

Send the form at the bottom of this page or message us on WhatsApp. We reply personally, book a short call, and send a written plan with the scope and price before any work starts.
