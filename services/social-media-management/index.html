FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp. A person replies, not a bot.

What does social media management include?

A monthly content calendar, post and carousel design, reels, captions, scheduling, community management, paid boosting and a monthly report. One team handles all of it, so your feed looks and sounds like one brand.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube. We focus on the platforms where your buyers actually are: LinkedIn for B2B, Instagram and Facebook for food and retail, TikTok for reach.

How many posts do we get a month?

It depends on your goals and package. As an example, we plan 12 posts a month for Shangrilla Gardens & BBQ, plus campaigns for launches, offers and festivals.

Do you shoot the content or do we send it?

We shoot it. Our own crew handles photos and reels, and our designers make every post, so you are not stuck finding content each week.

Do you manage brands outside Pakistan?

Yes. We plan, design and schedule for brands in any time zone, with captions in the language your audience uses, and we report on the same monthly cycle.

How much does social media management cost?

Our packages start from PKR 120,000 a month. The price depends on platforms, posts, reels and whether we shoot on site. Send the form and we will suggest the right package.
