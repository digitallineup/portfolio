Digital Lineup is a Meta ads agency in Multan, Pakistan. We run Facebook and Instagram ads for brands in Pakistan and worldwide, and we make the ads ourselves: the videos, the posters, the landing pages and the tracking. Nothing gets lost between the strategy and the ad account.

Quick answers

What does a Meta ads agency do?

Plans the campaigns, sets up tracking, makes the ad creative, builds audiences, manages the budget daily and reports what each rupee or dollar produced. We also build the landing page the ads send people to.

Do I need to give you my ad account?

No. You own your Business Manager, ad account and pixel, and pay Meta directly. We are added as a partner. If we ever part ways, you keep everything.

How long before Meta ads work?

The first month is mostly tracking, structure and testing. For an installation business we worked with, leads went from 9 in the first month to 71 in the second and 135 by the fifth.

What we do in your ad account

  • Tracking first: a pixel and conversions that record real leads and sales, not just clicks.
  • Campaigns split by service: so you can see which product or service actually makes money.
  • Creative that sells: before and after, real results and short reels, made in-house.
  • Lookalike audiences: built from people who actually became leads.
  • Landing pages: fast, phone-first pages with a short form and reviews up top.
  • Monthly report: leads, cost per lead, spend and the plan for next month.
