FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp. A person replies, not a bot.

What is included in a brand identity?

A logo and mark, colour palette, typography, graphic elements and a brand book that shows how to use them. Depending on the business we add signage, packaging, menus, uniforms, stationery, social templates, a logo film and launch content.

How long does a branding project take?

Most identities take two to four weeks: brief and research, two or three concept directions shown as real mockups, refinement, then final files and the brand book. A larger system with packaging or interiors can take longer, and we agree the timeline before we start.

How much does brand identity design cost?

Brand identity projects start from PKR 120,000. The final price depends on how many touchpoints you need, for example signage, packaging, a mascot or a logo film. Tell us what you need in the form below and we will send the right package with a fixed price.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

Yes. Our studio is in Multan and we work with clients remotely over video calls and shared boards. Final files are delivered digitally, ready for print and screen wherever your business is.

Can you refresh my existing logo instead of starting over?

Yes. If your name and mark already have recognition, we often keep what works and modernise the rest: cleaner geometry, a proper colour and type system, and a brand book so it stays consistent.

What files do I get at the end?

Print and screen versions of every logo (vector and PNG), colour codes, fonts, templates and the brand book as a PDF, so your printer, your team and any future agency can use the identity correctly.

Can you launch the brand after the identity is done?

Yes, and most clients ask us to. Because production, social media and ads are in-house, we can shoot launch content, make the logo film and run the launch campaign with the same team that designed the brand.
