FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp. A person replies, not a bot.

What does a Meta ads agency do?

Agrees the goal with you first (enquiries, reach or followers), sets up tracking, makes the ad creative, builds the audiences, manages the budget every day and reports the one number that goal is measured by, plus what it cost. We also build the landing page or WhatsApp flow the ads send people to.

Which goal should my ads have?

If you sell something people enquire about, like solar, education, property or services, the goal is conversations or leads. If you are launching a product, a menu or a campaign film, the goal is reach and views. If you are building a brand page from zero, the goal is followers. Most clients run one main goal and a small second one, and we tell you which before spending anything.

Do I need to give you my ad account?

No. You own your Business Manager, ad account and pixel, and you pay Meta directly. We are added as a partner, and if we ever part ways you keep everything, including the audiences we built.

How long before Meta ads start working?

Reach and engagement campaigns show results in the first week. Lead campaigns need a test month: for one installation business, leads went from 9 in the first month to 71 in the second and 135 by the fifth, while cost per lead fell by three quarters.

Do you run ads outside Pakistan?

Yes. We run campaigns in Pakistan and abroad, in rupees, dollars or any other currency. The creative is made by our own team, so ads can be tested and refreshed weekly in any market.

What budget do I need for ads?

It depends on the goal and the market. In Pakistan, a WhatsApp conversation from a solar or education ad has cost our clients between 4 and 9 US cents, and a page follow under one rupee. We suggest a test budget for the first month, then scale what works. Our management packages start from PKR 120,000 and ad spend is paid to Meta separately.

Do you make the ad creative too?

Yes, and that is the point. Our designers, photographers and video crew make every ad in-house, so we can test new hooks every week instead of waiting on another agency.
