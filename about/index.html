Our story

One team.
Brief to numbers.

Digital Lineup started four years ago with a simple idea: a business should not need three different vendors for a video, a logo and an ad campaign. It should be able to brief one team, see the work at every stage and get the numbers at the end.

For most of those four years we worked remotely with clients across Pakistan and abroad. In September 2026 we opened our first physical office and studio in Multan, so clients can walk in, have chai, see the work on our screens and meet the people who will make theirs.

Today the studio covers food and product photography, video production, creator campaigns, Meta ads, social media management, branding and websites. The same people plan the campaign, shoot it, design it and run it, so nothing gets lost between the idea and the ad account.

Some of the results we can show: an Apex Solar creator film that reached 11 million views, a Facebook page grown past 150K followers in eight months for Solar Mandee, and an installation business taken from 9 to 135 leads a month with Meta ads. Read more from the founder, Ali Hannan Siddique.
