Why most restaurant marketing fails

Restaurants post a lot and sell little, because the photo was taken on a phone at 3pm, the caption was written in a hurry and the ad was boosted to "people near you" with no offer. The dish looked fine to the owner and invisible to a hungry stranger scrolling past.

Food is the one category where the photograph is the product. A plate shot under proper light with steam, texture and a clean background outperforms the same plate shot on a phone by a margin that shows up in orders, not likes. Everything we do for restaurants starts from that fact.
