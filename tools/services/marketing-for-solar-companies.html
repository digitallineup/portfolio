Why solar needs its own marketing playbook

Solar is a high-ticket, high-doubt purchase. The buyer has a bill that hurts, a WhatsApp full of quotes and no way to tell a good installer from a bad one. Generic "go green" ads do not move them. What moves them is proof: an install they can see, a bill before and after, a dealer or an engineer they can put a face to, and a number they can reply to.

That is why our solar work is built on film and creators first, and on ads second. The film creates the trust; the ad puts it in front of the right homes.

What we do for solar companies

  • Creator and influencer films: the Apex Solar film that reached 11M views was a creator walking through a real install.
  • Product and install photography: batteries, inverters, panels and finished rooftops, shot for the catalogue, the website and the ads.
  • Meta lead campaigns: WhatsApp and form leads from homeowners and businesses, with the creative tested weekly and cost per lead reported monthly.
  • Dealer and expo content: stands, dealer meetings and launches filmed and turned into social content the same week.
  • Social media management that keeps the page alive between campaigns, so a visitor who checks you out finds a company, not a dead feed.
