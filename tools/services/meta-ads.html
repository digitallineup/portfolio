How we think about it

Most ad accounts fail because nobody agreed what the ads were for. We start with one goal: conversations, reach or followers. Then we build the creative for that goal, track the one number it is measured by, and move the budget every week towards whatever is winning.

What we do

Every campaign we run falls into one of these. The goal decides the creative, the audience and the number we report.

Goal 01

Conversations & leads

For businesses people enquire about: solar, education, property, services, B2B. Ads open a WhatsApp or Messenger chat, or a short lead form, and we track every one to a sale.

Reach & launches
