Process

From brief to brand book.

You see your brand in the real world before you approve it: on the sign, the box and the phone screen. Not on a mood board.

Week 1

Brief & research

One working session on your business, buyers and competitors. We write it up as a one-page brief you sign off.

  • Brief
  • Competitor audit
  • Positioning
Week 1 to 2

Concept directions

Two or three distinct directions, each shown on real mockups: signage, packaging, social posts and the app icon.

  • 2 to 3 directions
  • Photoreal mockups
Week 2 to 3

Refine the chosen one

We develop the direction you pick: the final mark, colour and type system, patterns and every application you need.

  • Final mark
  • System
  • Applications
Week 3 to 4

Brand book & launch

Final files for print and screen, the brand book, and if you want it, a logo film and launch content from our production team.

  • Brand book
  • Source files
  • Logo film
  • Launch content
