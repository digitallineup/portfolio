What Ali does at Digital Lineup

Strategy on every account, the ad accounts themselves, and the standard. The team shoots, designs and edits; Ali decides what gets made and reads the numbers every morning. He is the person a client calls when something is not working, and the person who says no to a brief that will not work.

How the agency was built

Digital Lineup started in 2022 as a one-person operation doing social media for local brands and grew entirely by referral: every client so far came from another client. In September 2026 the team moved into its first office at Al Shifa Hospital on Masoom Shah Road, Multan. The five people on the about page are the whole company.

What he writes about

The agency blog is where the method goes: how to choose a digital marketing agency in Pakistan, what a full in-house team looks like, and the playbooks behind each reel the team posts. Every post is written from a client account, with the numbers.
