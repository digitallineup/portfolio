What parents actually respond to

Parents do not choose a school from a poster of the building. They respond to faces: teachers, students, a classroom in use, a result on a board. They respond to a clear answer to "what does it cost and when can I visit". And they respond on WhatsApp, not on a web form they have to find.

Every admissions campaign we run is built on that: real campus content, an offer a parent can act on, and a conversation that starts in the app they already use.

What we do for schools and colleges

  • Admissions campaigns on Facebook and Instagram, targeted by area and timed to intake dates, reported by conversations and cost per conversation.
  • Campus films and photography: classrooms, labs, sports, events and results, shot by our crew and edited the same week.
  • Social media management through the year, so the page is alive when parents look, not only in admissions season.
  • Prospectus, signage and identity for new campuses and rebrands.
  • Event coverage: results days, sports days, annual functions, turned into content within days.
