Why B2B buyers ignore most manufacturer marketing

A distributor, a procurement manager or an export buyer is not persuaded by a logo animation and a slogan. They want to see the plant, the process, the capacity, the certifications and the people. Most manufacturers in Pakistan show none of it, so the buyer defaults to the supplier with the best WhatsApp catalogue.

Our B2B work starts with the film: the plant from the air, the line in motion, the quality checks, the warehouse. Everything else, the LinkedIn posts, the website, the brochure, is cut from that.

What we do for manufacturers and B2B companies
