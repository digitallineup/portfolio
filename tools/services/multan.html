What a marketing agency in Multan should give you

Multan has plenty of pages that call themselves digital marketing agencies: freelancers, Facebook pages, software houses with a marketing tab. Very few have a crew that can be at your shop on Tuesday, a designer who makes your posts and your ads, and someone who reads the ad account every morning. That is what we built, in one room, so nothing is lost between a photographer, a designer and an ads person who have never met.

If you are comparing agencies in Multan, ask every one of them the same five things: show me three results with numbers, who shoots and who designs, who owns the ad account, who answers my WhatsApp on a Sunday, and where are your reviews. Our answers are on this page.

Why Multan businesses choose an in-house team

The usual Multan arrangement is a page manager who outsources the design to Lahore and the video to a student. It works until the first launch, when the photo, the post and the ad all need to land in the same week. With one team, the person who shot the dish designs the post and the person who designed the post runs the ad. You brief once, on WhatsApp, and you get one monthly report with the numbers that matter: reach, leads, cost per conversation, sales where we can track them.

We also know the city. We know which areas respond to which offers, when Ramadan and Eid campaigns need to start, which creators in Multan actually move footfall, and what a restaurant on Bosan Road needs that one in Cantt does not.
