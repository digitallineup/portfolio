Digital Lineup is a video production company in Multan. Our own crew plans, shoots and edits every film, so the idea you approve is the film that ships. We make brand films, reels for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, product films, drone shoots and creator campaigns for brands across Pakistan.

## Quick answers

### What kind of videos does Digital Lineup make?

Brand films, short reels, product films with 3D and motion, drone and on-site shoots, food films, behind-the-scenes content and creator or celebrity campaign films. Everything is shot and edited in-house by our team in Multan.

### Do you shoot outside Multan?

Yes. We shoot on location across Pakistan. For Apex Solar we ran production for influencer shoots in Multan and Karachi, and for Boota Brothers we shot on site at their flour mills.

### How fast do we get the edit?

Most reels are edited within the same week as the shoot. Larger brand films follow the brief, concept, production and launch steps below, usually over two to four weeks.
