Most agencies sell twelve services and are average at ten. We kept the four that actually move a brand and built a team around each one. The same people plan, make and run the work, so nothing gets lost between the strategy and the ad account.

## Quick answers

### What services does Digital Lineup offer?

Four: video and content production, performance marketing (Meta and Google ads), brand identity, and social media management. All four are made in-house by our team in Multan, Pakistan.

### Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

Yes. We work with brands across Pakistan and with businesses in Australia, including TV mounting and picture hanging installers. Our team works through the Australian afternoon and evening.

### Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. You pay Meta or Google directly from your own ad account, and our fee covers the work. That way you can see exactly where every rupee or dollar goes.

## 1. Video and content production

Brand films, reels, product shoots and creator campaigns. Our own crew, lights and camera, on site where it matters, edited in-house the same week.

- Brand films and campaign films
- Reels and short-form video for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok
- Creator and influencer campaigns
- Product, food and drone shoots

**Proof:** one [Apex Solar](/work/apex-solar.html) creator film reached 11 million views. See also our work for [Boota Brothers](/work/boota-brothers.html) and [Hamid Enterprises](/work/hamid-enterprises.html).

## 2. Performance marketing

Meta and Google ads with the creative, landing pages and pixel work done by the same team.

- Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads and Google ads
- Pixel and conversion tracking set up properly
- Lookalike audiences built from real leads
- Landing pages that work on a phone
- A monthly report you can read in two minutes

**Proof:** a Perth TV mounting and picture hanging business went from 9 to 135 leads a month, with cost per lead down from about A$120 to under A$35. [Read the full case study](/blog/meta-ads-for-tv-mounting-businesses/). Installers in Australia can see our [packages for installers](/installers/).

## 3. Brand identity

Logo, brand system, signage, packaging and the brand book that holds it all together, for businesses that are about to be seen by a lot more people.

- Logo and visual system
- Brand book and guidelines
- Signage and packaging
- Mascots and 3D brand characters

**Proof:** full identities for [Capxa](/work/capxa.html), [Lume Beauty Studio](/work/lume.html), [Spice & Grill](/work/spice-and-grill.html) and the mascot for [Shan Electronics](/work/shan-electronics.html).

## 4. Social media management

Daily content, design and community for brands that want a feed people actually follow.

- Monthly content calendar
- Post and reel design
- Community management
- Paid boosting and monthly reporting

**Proof:** Solar Mandee's Facebook page grew past 150K followers in eight months. See our food work for [Shangrilla](/work/shangrilla-bakers.html) and B2B content for [Rainbow Printing Solutions](/work/rainbow-printing.html).
