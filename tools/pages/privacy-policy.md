This policy covers the website digitallineup.com, run by Digital Lineup, Office #1, Basement, Al Shifa Hospital, Masoom Shah Rd, Multan, Pakistan. Questions: [hello@digitallineup.com](mailto:hello@digitallineup.com).

## Information you give us

When you fill in a form on this website, we receive what you type, such as your name, email, phone number, business name, website and message. We use it only to reply to you and to discuss the work you asked about. It is stored in our private Google account and seen only by our team.

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how people use the website, for example which pages are visited, how far people scroll and where they click. These tools use cookies or similar technology and may collect your device type, browser, approximate location and the pages you visit. Clarity may record anonymised sessions of how the page is used. We use this only to improve the website.

You can read how these companies handle data in the [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Microsoft privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). You can block cookies in your browser settings, and the website will still work.

## Links to other websites

Our pages link to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok and Google Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies.

## Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us through a form. Email [hello@digitallineup.com](mailto:hello@digitallineup.com) and we will handle it.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
