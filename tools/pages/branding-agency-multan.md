Digital Lineup is a branding agency in Multan. We design the logo, the colours, the type and the brand book, then carry it onto signage, packaging, uniforms and social media, so your brand looks like one business everywhere a customer meets it.

## Quick answers

### What is included in a brand identity?

A logo and mark, colour palette, typography, brand patterns and graphic elements, and a brand book that shows how to use them. Depending on the business we add signage, packaging, menus, uniforms, stationery and launch content.

### How long does a branding project take?

Most identities take two to four weeks: brief and research, two or three concept directions shown as real mockups, refinement, then the final files and brand book.

### Do you only work with businesses in Multan?

No. Our studio is in Multan, and we have built identities for brands across Pakistan, including an agency, a beauty studio and a restaurant.

## What we design

- **Logo and mark:** a mark that works at the size of an app icon and the size of a shop sign.
- **Brand system:** colours, type, patterns and rules that keep everything consistent.
- **Brand book:** one document your team, printers and future agencies can follow.
- **Signage and interiors:** shop fronts, wall graphics and interior mockups.
- **Packaging and menus:** boxes, bags, labels and menu systems.
- **Mascots and 3D characters:** a face for the brand, like the Shan Electronics mascot.
