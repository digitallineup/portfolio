Digital Lineup is a social media management agency in Multan working with brands across Pakistan. We plan the month, design every post, shoot the reels, manage comments and messages, boost what works and send you a report you can read in two minutes.

## Quick answers

### What does social media management include?

A monthly content calendar, post and carousel design, reels, captions, scheduling, community management, paid boosting and a monthly report. One team handles all of it, so your feed looks and sounds like one brand.

### Which platforms do you manage?

Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube. We pick the platforms where your buyers actually are: LinkedIn for B2B, Instagram and Facebook for food and retail, Facebook ads for lead generation.

### How many posts a month do we get?

It depends on the package and your goals. For example, we plan 12 posts a month for Shangrilla Gardens & BBQ, alongside campaigns for launches and festivals.

## What you get each month

- **Content calendar:** a month planned around your offers, launches and the local calendar (Eid, Ramadan, national days, the weather).
- **Design:** posts, carousels and stories in your brand, made by our in-house designers.
- **Reels:** short videos shot and edited by our own crew. See our [video production](/services/video-production-multan/).
- **Community management:** replies to comments and messages, so no lead goes cold.
- **Paid boosting:** a small budget behind the posts that work, run from your own ad account.
- **Monthly report:** reach, engagement, followers, leads and what we change next month.
