## From computer science to marketing

I come from computer science, not advertising. That shaped how I run an agency more than anything else. I look at a campaign the way an engineer looks at a system: what goes in, what comes out, and which single variable is worth changing next.

It is also why I care so much about tracking. A Meta campaign without proper conversion tracking is a system with no feedback loop. Most of the biggest improvements we have made for clients came from fixing what the ad platform could see, before touching a single creative.

## Building Digital Lineup

Digital Lineup started with a simple belief: a business in Pakistan should not need three vendors for a video, a logo and an ad campaign. It should be able to brief one team, see the work at every stage and get the numbers at the end.

For four years we grew without a single paid ad for ourselves. Every client came through a referral, and some of the brands we started with are still with us today. In September 2026 we opened our first physical office and studio in Multan, so clients can walk in, have chai and see the work on our screens.

Today the team is six people: content production, media, design, project management and strategy, all in-house.

## How I work

- **The numbers sit next to the work.** If we cannot show what a campaign did, we do not call it a result.
- **No fake results, no fake content.** We shoot the real product and report the real figures, even when a month goes the wrong way.
- **One idea per campaign.** A launch with one strong idea beats a price list every time.
- **The founder stays on the account.** Clients talk to the person who makes the decisions, not an inbox.
